School streets change more than the school run

The intervention is almost absurdly simple, which is part of why it is resisted.

Air quality improves at the gate, obviously. Less obviously, walking and cycling rates rise across the whole catchment, and they stay up after the novelty passes.

Objections concentrate in the first few weeks and then largely disappear. Schemes that survive their first term almost never get reversed.

The lesson for anyone trying to start one: plan for eight weeks of complaints, and do not treat them as a verdict.

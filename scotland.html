Scotland

Advancing place-based public health, lifestyle medicine, and community resilience across Scottish communities.

In Scotland, Healthier UK partners with local authorities, NHS Scotland boards, and community organisations to champion health creation across urban centres and rural regions.

Scottish Health Priorities

  • Deep Integration: Linking social care, housing, and NHS boards directly with third-sector community anchors.
  • Outdoor & Nature Prescribing: Harnessing Scotland's natural environment to promote mental wellbeing and active living.
  • Tackling Inequalities: Focusing resources on areas facing severe health disparities to ensure preventative infrastructure reaches all communities.

Get Involved in Scotland: If you are a Scottish organisation or practitioner interested in joining our Scottish network, please contact our team.

Creating the conditions for a healthier UK through place-based health creation, community empowerment, and cross-government policy reform.

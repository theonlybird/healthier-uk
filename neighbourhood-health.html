Neighbourhood Health

Giving neighbourhood health genuine purpose: health creation led by places and communities.

Beyond Relocating Top-Down Services

The national transition towards neighbourhood health is the right instinct — but it must amount to more than simply relocating top-down clinical services into community venues. The defining opportunity is to give neighbourhood health real purpose: health creation, led by places and communities.

The Pillars of Authentic Neighbourhood Health

•

Relational Capacity

Prioritising human relationships, trusted community champions, and peer networks that build genuine confidence and resilience.

•

Shared Community Delivery

Co-designing services with residents, faith groups, sports clubs, and cultural anchors rather than imposing rigid institutional models.

•

Strengthening Human Connection

Deploying digital tools that support human relationships and community connection rather than replacing them.

In England, the health service faces unprecedented pressures. Demand and acute spending keep escalating while working-age health falters. Healthier UK works with national policymakers, Mayoral Combined authorities, NHS Integrated Care Systems (ICSs), and local authorities to establish health creation as a core operating model.

Rather than simply treating illness after it occurs, English health policy must invest in the foundational conditions: active transport, green space, warm homes, good work, and social connection.

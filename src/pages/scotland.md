## Scottish health priorities

- **Deep Integration:** Linking social care, housing, and NHS boards directly with third-sector community anchors.
- **Outdoor & Nature Prescribing:** Harnessing Scotland's natural environment to promote mental wellbeing and active living.
- **Tackling Inequalities:** Focusing resources on areas facing severe health disparities to ensure preventative infrastructure reaches all communities.

## Mayoral leadership in health creation

Mayoral Combined authorities (MCAs) hold the levers that shape daily living: regional transport networks, spatial planning, housing standards, adult skills, and economic development. By integrating health into all regional policies, Mayors are proving that a healthier population directly creates a more productive workforce and a more resilient local economy.

![Mayoral combined authority health demonstrators](assets/images/feature-activity.jpeg)

## The Prevention Demonstrators Model

Healthier UK works with combined authorities across the country to establish Mayoral prevention demonstrators. Rather than short-term pilots that evaporate when seed grants end, we support regions in adopting long-term operating models where savings generated from reduced NHS and welfare demand are reinvested into grassroots community assets.

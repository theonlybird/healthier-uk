## Key focus areas in Northern Ireland

- **Community-Led Health Development:** Supporting neighbourhood health champions and voluntary associations.
- **Mental Wellbeing & Trauma-Informed Care:** Embedding holistic wellbeing support into place-based settings.
- **Cross-Sector Collaboration:** Aligning health and social care trusts with local district councils and sports/arts networks.
